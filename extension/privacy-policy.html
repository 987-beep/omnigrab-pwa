Privacy Policy for OmniGrab Pro

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Single Purpose & Data Collection Philosophy

OmniGrab Pro ("The Extension & PWA") operates with a strict Zero Data Collection policy. The extension exists solely to assist users in identifying and downloading media streams, photos, and audio files that they actively choose to download.

2. Data Handling & Permissions

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, browsing history, or keystrokes.
  • activeTab Permission: Used only when the user opens the popup or clicks the download button to sniff media URLs on that active tab.
  • downloads Permission: Used solely to initiate the saving of media files to the user's local downloads folder.
  • storage Permission: Used locally on the user's device to store custom server preferences and user settings. No data is sent to external trackers.
  • contextMenus: Used to provide right-click download options on media elements.

3. Third-Party Sharing

OmniGrab Pro does not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit any user information to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

4. Contact & Support

For questions or support regarding OmniGrab Pro, please visit our GitHub repository or contact the development team directly.
