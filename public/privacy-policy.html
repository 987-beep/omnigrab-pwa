OmniGrab Pro Privacy & Storage Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

OmniGrab Pro is built on strict Personal Privacy Principles. We do not sell data, track browsing activities, or log personal IP addresses. Your downloads and bookmarks synchronize directly to your personal database.

1. Private Cloud Sync Architecture

OmniGrab Pro syncs directly with your Turso LibSQL database:

  • No Multi-User Mixing: Your downloads and saved items stay in your own private cloud instance.
  • Seamless Cross-Device Sync: Your Android PWA, Desktop Browser, and Chrome Extension sync with your personal database.

2. 02:00 AM Midnight Auto-Pruning Engine

To keep the database storage within free tier limits, an automated engine runs daily at 02:00 AM:

🗑️ Ephemeral Data Purged Daily at 02:00 AM

  • downloads_history older than 24 hours
  • Temporary video extraction metadata & thumbnail buffers

🛡️ Permanent Data (Preserved Forever - Never Deleted)

  • saved_bookmarks (Your starred videos and media links)
  • user_settings (Quality presets, preferred formats)

3. Direct Stream Downloader Protocol

OmniGrab Pro processes media streams directly without intermediate permanent file hoarding. Audio conversion and video trimming occur on-the-fly.
